Privacy Policy — Last updated September 12, 2026

Signal Space Fighter is developed and published by Mantas Žemeckas.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use Signal Space Fighter.

Advertising

Signal Space Fighter uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google.

The game may offer optional rewarded advertisements. Players may choose to watch these advertisements in exchange for in-game benefits. Watching a rewarded advertisement is optional.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, interactions with advertisements, diagnostic information, and other identifiers or technical information necessary to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising services.

Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on the user's location, consent choices, age-related requirements, and applicable law.

Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by law, including in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Signal Space Fighter uses Google's consent system to request the user's privacy and advertising choices before requesting advertisements that require consent.

Users may be able to choose between available advertising and privacy options presented by Google.

Signal Space Fighter will provide access to privacy and advertising choices from within the game when required, allowing users to review or change their consent choices.

Information Stored by the Game

Signal Space Fighter may store game-related information locally on the user's device, such as game progress, settings, unlocked content, high scores, and other gameplay preferences.

This locally stored gameplay information is used to operate the game and remember the player's settings and progress. The developer does not operate a Signal Space Fighter user-account system or game server that receives this local save information.

Uninstalling the game or clearing its application data may remove locally stored game information.

Third-Party Services

Signal Space Fighter uses Google AdMob and related Google advertising and consent technologies.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations. Their data handling practices are controlled by the respective service providers.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners as necessary to provide advertising, measure advertisement performance, prevent fraud and abuse, comply with legal obligations, and provide related advertising services.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to limit data handling by Signal Space Fighter to what is necessary for the game's functionality and integrated services.

Information processed by third-party services is subject to the security practices of those service providers.

Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information stored locally by Signal Space Fighter remains on the user's device until it is overwritten, application data is cleared, or the game is uninstalled.

Signal Space Fighter does not currently provide user accounts or maintain a developer-operated database containing player profiles.

Data processed by Google or other advertising providers is retained and deleted according to those providers' respective policies and applicable law.

For privacy questions concerning Signal Space Fighter or requests relating to information directly controlled by the developer, users may contact the developer using the email address below.

Children's Privacy

Signal Space Fighter is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children through a developer-operated account or server.

Advertising and consent behavior may be adjusted where required by applicable laws, platform policies, and the age group selected for distribution of the game.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Signal Space Fighter's features, advertising services, data practices, or legal requirements change. Changes will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
